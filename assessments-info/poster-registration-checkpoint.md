# Poster Registration Checkpoint

**Weight:** 0% of overall grade (formative)
**Format:** Register the paper you plan to present your poster on, via Moodle
**Date:** Friday 30 October 2026

## Aim

To get students thinking about the poster in good time, and to allow the lecturer to read papers and give feedback.  This lets me read check to make sure that your paper is reasonable, and gives me time to read it if I don't know it.  

## Notes

This is the first of three linked components of the overall Poster assessment (50% of overall grade):
- Poster registration checkpoint - 0%, formative (this document)
- [Poster submission](poster-submission.md) - 40%
- [Poster day with live poster question](poster-day.md) - 10%
